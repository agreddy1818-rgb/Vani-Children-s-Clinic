Dr. Sreevani Reddy

Consultant Pediatrician & Neonatologist

10+ Years of Experience

Dr. Sreevani Reddy is an experienced Pediatrician and Neonatologist dedicated to providing quality healthcare for newborns and children. She is an MD Pediatrics Gold Medalist from Osmania Medical College, Hyderabad, with specialized fellowship training in Neonatology.

She previously worked as a Senior Resident at Gandhi Hospital, Hyderabad, and as a Consultant at Medicover Women and Child Hospitals, Hyderabad.

Blood Tests & Diagnostics

Blood Tests & Diagnostics

Common tests a healthcare professional may recommend.

Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Helps assess blood cells and possible anemia or infection.

Hemoglobin Test

Measures hemoglobin levels and can help assess anemia.

Blood Glucose

Measures blood sugar when recommended by a clinician.

Thyroid Function

Tests such as TSH may help assess thyroid function.

Liver Function

Blood tests used to assess aspects of liver function.

Kidney Function

Tests that may help assess kidney function.

Important: Not every child needs these tests. The doctor recommends appropriate tests based on the child's needs. Contact the clinic to confirm which tests are available.
